Mobile data will not turn onThe toggle does not switch on, or data is on but nothing loads.
  1. Turn on Airplane mode for 30 seconds, then turn it off.
  2. Check that your data bundle is active. Dial your network's balance code (for example *124# on MTN).
  3. Open Settings > SIM cards (or Connections > SIM manager). Make sure the SIM is enabled and selected for Mobile data.
  4. Open Settings > Connections > Mobile networks > Access Point Names, tap the menu and choose Reset to default.
  5. Open Settings > Connections > Data usage and turn off Data saver and any data limit.
  6. In Mobile networks > Network mode, choose the 4G/3G/2G (auto) option.
  7. Restart the phone. If it still fails, take the SIM out, clean it, put it back, or test it in another phone.
  8. If nothing has worked, go to Settings > General management > Reset > Reset network settings. This clears saved Wi-Fi and Bluetooth pairings, not your files.
Phone is stuck on the logo or keeps restartingThe phone shows the brand logo, then restarts again and again.
  1. Hold the power button for 10 to 15 seconds until the phone switches off. Charge it for at least 30 minutes, then switch it on.
  2. If it still loops, switch it off and boot into recovery. On most phones this is Power + Volume Up held together. Samsung, Tecno, Infinix and Itel use slightly different combinations, so search your model if this one does nothing.
  3. In recovery, use the volume keys to move and the power key to select. Choose Wipe cache partition. This does not delete your files.
  4. Restart with Reboot system now.
  5. If it still loops, start in Safe mode (if the phone reaches the lock screen at all) and uninstall the last app you added.
  6. Factory reset from recovery erases the phone. Do this only after you have tried the steps above, and bring the phone to us first if the photos and contacts matter.

A factory reset removes everything on the phone. If you have no backup, contact us before you try it.

Phone is slow or storage is fullApps lag, the camera stops saving, or you see a storage warning.
  1. Open Settings > Battery and device care > Storage (the name differs by brand) to see what is using space.
  2. Delete large videos and old downloads. Check the Downloads folder in the Files app.
  3. In WhatsApp go to Settings > Storage and data > Manage storage and remove big videos and forwarded media.
  4. Back up photos to Google Photos, then delete the local copies.
  5. Go to Settings > Apps, sort by size, and uninstall apps you do not use. Disable pre-installed apps you never open.
  6. Clear cache for the heaviest apps: App info > Storage > Clear cache.
  7. Restart the phone. If it is still slow with free space, check for a system update in Settings > Software update.
Phone gets hot or the battery drains quicklyThe phone warms up while idle, or the battery is empty by midday.
  1. Open Settings > Battery and see which apps use the most power.
  2. Close or uninstall apps that use a lot of battery in the background.
  3. Turn down screen brightness and set a short screen timeout.
  4. Turn off Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and location when you are not using them.
  5. Take the case off while charging and avoid charging in direct sun.
  6. Use the charger and cable that came with the phone, or one of the same rating.
  7. If the battery is old (over two years of daily use) or the back of the phone is swelling, stop using it and bring it in for a battery replacement.
Play Store will not download or update appsYou see Pending, Download error, or the store will not open.
  1. Check that you have internet by opening a website.
  2. Check that the phone's date, time and time zone are correct (set them to automatic).
  3. Go to Settings > Apps > Google Play Store > Storage and tap Clear cache, then Clear data.
  4. Do the same for Google Play Services.
  5. Make sure there is free storage on the phone.
  6. Remove your Google account in Settings > Accounts, restart, then sign in again.
  7. Restart the phone and try the download again.
Lock down your phone's securitySettings worth changing once on any Android phone.
  1. Set a screen lock: Settings > Security > Screen lock. Use a PIN of 6 or more digits, not 1234 or your birth year.
  2. Turn on Find My Device: Settings > Security > Find My Device.
  3. Update the phone: Settings > Software update > Download and install.
  4. Open Play Store > profile icon > Play Protect > Settings and enable Scan apps with Play Protect.
  5. Keep Install unknown apps off: Settings > Apps > Special access > Install unknown apps. Allow it briefly only for a source you trust.
  6. Add a recovery phone number and email to your Google account.
  7. Do not share OTP codes, PINs or your mobile money PIN with anyone, including people who say they are from the network.
Control app permissionsStop apps from reading contacts, location or the microphone when they do not need to.
  1. Open Settings > Security and privacy > Privacy > Permission manager.
  2. Tap a permission such as Location, Camera, Microphone or Contacts to see which apps use it.
  3. Tap an app and choose Don't allow, or Allow only while using the app for location.
  4. A torch, calculator or wallpaper app does not need your contacts or SMS. Remove those permissions or uninstall the app.
  5. For one app: press and hold its icon > App info > Permissions.
  6. In App info, turn on Remove permissions if app isn't used.
  7. Check Settings > Apps > Special access > Display over other apps and Device admin apps. Turn off anything you do not recognise.
Vidmate or another APK took over my phoneApps open strange websites after you installed an APK from outside the Play Store.
  1. Do not tap the ads or sites that appear. Turn on Airplane mode so nothing more downloads.
  2. Restart in Safe mode: hold the power button, then press and hold Power off and tap OK. Third-party apps are disabled in Safe mode.
  3. Go to Settings > Security > Device admin apps. Turn off anything unfamiliar or named Update, Service or System.
  4. Go to Settings > Accessibility > Installed apps and turn off any service you did not install on purpose.
  5. Go to Settings > Apps and uninstall Vidmate and any app you installed around the same time. Use the filter to show system apps and look for odd names.
  6. Go to Settings > Apps > Special access > Display over other apps and remove unknown apps.
  7. Reset defaults: Settings > Apps > Choose default apps. Set your usual Home app and browser, then clear the browser's data.
  8. Open Play Store > Play Protect > Scan and remove anything it flags.
  9. Restart normally. If the problem comes back, back up photos and contacts, then factory reset: Settings > General management > Reset > Factory data reset. Afterwards, install apps only from the Play Store.
