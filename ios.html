iPhone will not connect to mobile data or Wi-FiNo signal, no data, or Wi-Fi keeps dropping.
  1. Swipe down from the top-right corner, turn Airplane mode on for 30 seconds, then off.
  2. Open Settings > Mobile Data (or Cellular). Make sure Mobile Data is on and the apps you need are allowed.
  3. Restart: hold the side button and a volume button, drag to power off, then turn it on again.
  4. Check for a carrier update: Settings > General > About. If a prompt appears, tap Update.
  5. For Wi-Fi: Settings > Wi-Fi > tap the (i) next to the network > Forget This Network, then join again.
  6. Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Reset > Reset Network Settings.
  7. If it still fails, test your SIM in another phone, or contact your network.
iPhone is frozen or stuck on the Apple logoThe screen does not respond, or the phone stays on the logo.
  1. Force restart. On iPhone 8 and newer: press and release Volume Up, press and release Volume Down, then hold the Side button until the Apple logo appears.
  2. On iPhone 7: hold Volume Down and the Side button together until the logo appears.
  3. On iPhone 6s and older: hold the Home button and the Top (or Side) button together until the logo appears.
  4. If the phone still sits on the logo, charge it for 30 minutes and try again.
  5. Connect the phone to a computer with a cable. On a Mac use Finder; on Windows use the Apple Devices app or iTunes.
  6. Put the phone in recovery mode (repeat the force restart but keep holding the Side button after the logo appears, until the connect-to-computer screen shows).
  7. When the computer offers it, choose Update. This reinstalls iOS without erasing your data. Choose Restore only as a last option because it erases the phone.

Restore erases everything. Back up first if you can, or bring the phone to us.

Free up storage when your iPhone is fullFix the Storage Almost Full warning without losing what you need.
  1. Open Settings > General > iPhone Storage and wait for the list to load.
  2. Tap an app near the top. Choose Offload App to free space but keep its data, or Delete App to remove everything.
  3. Open WhatsApp > Settings > Storage and data > Manage storage and delete big videos you no longer need.
  4. Open Photos > Albums > Utilities > Recently Deleted > Select > Delete All.
  5. Back up photos to iCloud or Google Photos first, then remove the local copies.
  6. Go to Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data.
  7. Restart the phone so iOS can clear temporary files.
Battery drains fast or the phone gets warmThe battery drops quickly, or the phone is hot when idle.
  1. Open Settings > Battery and look at the usage list for the last 24 hours and 10 days.
  2. Turn on Low Power Mode from the same screen when the battery is low.
  3. Go to Settings > General > Background App Refresh and turn it off for apps that do not need it.
  4. Turn down brightness or turn on Auto-Brightness in Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size.
  5. Update iOS: Settings > General > Software Update.
  6. Check Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging. If maximum capacity is under 80%, a battery replacement will help.
  7. Avoid charging under a pillow or in direct sun, and use an Apple-certified cable.
App Store downloads or iOS update stuckThe download circle will not finish, or the update does not start.
  1. Check that you have internet and enough free storage.
  2. Press and hold the stuck app, tap Pause Download, then Resume Download.
  3. Restart the phone and try again.
  4. Sign out of the App Store: Settings > [your name] > Media & Purchases > Sign Out, then sign in again.
  5. Check Settings > General > Date & Time and turn on Set Automatically.
  6. For iOS updates, plug in the charger and join Wi-Fi. If there is not enough space, free some first (see the storage guide).
  7. If the update still fails, update from a computer using Finder or the Apple Devices app.
Secure your iPhone and Apple IDProtect your phone and account if it is lost or stolen.
  1. Settings > Face ID & Passcode > set a 6-digit or custom alphanumeric passcode.
  2. Settings > [your name] > Find My > Find My iPhone. Turn on Find My iPhone, Find My network and Send Last Location.
  3. Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security > turn on Two-Factor Authentication.
  4. Settings > General > Software Update > turn on Automatic Updates.
  5. Settings > Privacy & Security. Review Location Services, Camera, Microphone and Photos, and remove apps that do not need them.
  6. Do not tap links in texts saying your Apple ID is locked. Open Settings yourself instead.
  7. If the phone is lost, use another device at icloud.com/find, tap Mark as Lost and lock it.
